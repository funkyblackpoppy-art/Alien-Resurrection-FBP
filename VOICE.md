# Black Poppy Grimoire — Voice & Tone Guide

> *"The High Priestess does not beg to be believed. She knows, records, watches, and waits."*

---

## Who Is Speaking

The voice of Black Poppy Grimoire is **the practitioner who has already done the work.**

Not a guide. Not a teacher. Not a softened oracle hedging with "perhaps" and "you might consider." This is the voice of someone who has sat in the dark long enough to stop being afraid of it — and come back with language sharp enough to cut through the performance of spirituality.

The voice is feminine but not soft. Mystical but not vague. Direct but not cold. It carries the weight of a tradition without kneeling to one.

---

## Core Voice Qualities

### 1. Declarative, Not Decorative
Black Poppy says what is true without ornament. Every sentence earns its place.

**Do:**
> "Death is not doom. It is the door closing because the room is done."

**Don't:**
> "The Death card can sometimes be interpreted as a symbol of transformation and the beautiful inevitability of change in our lives."

---

### 2. Second Person — Direct Address
Speak to *you*, not *the querent* or *one.* The grimoire sees the reader. It does not perform objectivity.

**Do:**
> "Gather the reins. Your energy does not need to run feral to prove it is alive."

**Don't:**
> "This card often indicates a need for self-regulation in one's approach."

---

### 3. Short Sentences as Emphasis
After density, the short line lands like a stamp. Use it to close a thought, not open one.

**Do:**
> "Temperance says do not rush the alchemy. Some things curdle when forced."

**Do:**
> "The Star does not shout. It restores what survived."

**Don't:**
> "It is important to remember that patience is a virtue in this situation."

---

### 4. Earned Metaphor Only
Imagery must work. Nothing decorative. Nothing borrowed. Each metaphor has to do real labor — clarify, compress, or reveal something that plain language cannot.

**Do:** `"the spoke that is leaving"` (the Wheel of Fortune — something ending)
**Do:** `"put it in your hands and make it answer to your values"` (embodied power)
**Don't:** `"like a butterfly emerging from its chrysalis"` (decoration)
**Don't:** `"the sacred dance of life and death"` (borrowed mysticism)

---

### 5. The Scream Under the Silk
The aesthetic is parchment and ink — aged, quiet, dignified. But the voice has a hot-pink current running through it: a refusal to be decorative, a willingness to name the ugly thing directly.

The pink in the design *is* the scream. The voice carries it.

**Do:**
> "The Devil does not own you; it studies where you hand over the keys."

**Do:**
> "Justice says stop decorating the truth and sign your name under what is real."

**Don't:**
> "Sometimes we give away our power without realizing it."
> "Honesty is always the best policy in matters of the heart."

---

### 6. Sacred Without Apology
The practice is real. The cards are tools, not entertainment. The grimoire does not soften its authority to make skeptics comfortable. It does not over-explain the mythology. It does not perform enchantment — it simply is.

**Do:**
> "Go quiet enough to hear yourself, then come back with the lantern."

**Don't:**
> "Whether or not you believe in tarot, this card can serve as a helpful prompt for self-reflection."

---

## Tone Spectrum

The voice shifts register across different parts of the app. All of these are the same voice at different temperatures.

| Context | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Card readings — upright** | Warm authority | *"The Empress says beauty is not frivolous when it restores life."* |
| **Card readings — reversed** | Clear, unsparing | *"Reversed, it can point to carelessness... or treating chaos like a personality instead of a warning light."* |
| **blackPoppy lines** | Minimal. Stamped. Final. | *"The Sun says be seen without apologizing for the light."* |
| **Journal prompts** | Interrogative, intimate | *"Where am I being invited to take a wise risk?"* |
| **HowToRead lines** | Instructional, crisp | *"Read this card as a threshold. Look for beginnings, risk, innocence, freedom."* |
| **UI labels / eyebrows** | Stark, uppercase, zero-emotion | `LUNAR CURRENT` / `SACRED CHAOS` / `STRANGE RECORD` |
| **Navigation / actions** | Functional. No flourish. | `Enter the Grimoire` / `Draw Today's Card` / `Save Entry` |

---

## What Black Poppy Does Not Do

- **Does not hedge.** No "perhaps," "might," "could suggest," "it's possible that."
- **Does not apologize for mysticism.** No "whether or not you believe in" or "as a metaphor for."
- **Does not over-explain.** One sentence, well-chosen, is more powerful than three that explain themselves.
- **Does not perform warmth.** Care is in the precision, not the adjectives.
- **Does not use gold.** (This applies to the design spec too — gold is explicitly forbidden.)
- **Does not moralize.** It observes. It names. It does not lecture.
- **Does not speak in passive voice.** The cards act. You act. Something is happening.

## Sentence Structures to Reach For

```
[Card name] says [direct statement].
[Card name] does not [soft thing]. It [true thing].
[Card name] asks [a question that cuts].
[Card name] is not [the comfortable interpretation]. It is [the real one].
Read this card through [concrete noun], [concrete noun], and [the pressure point].
```

## blackPoppy Lines — The Standard

These are the shortest, highest-pressure lines in the app. One to two sentences. Subject → verb → consequence. No softening.

Each one should:
- Name the card without restating the standard meaning
- Say the thing the reader is avoiding
- End completely — no trailing clause

> The Empress says beauty is not frivolous when it restores life and returns you to your authority.

> The Emperor asks: what structure would make your power safer, cleaner, and harder to steal?

> Study the old systems, then build your own with teeth and grace.

> The Lovers asks what your desire costs and whether your values signed the agreement.

> Gather the reins. Your energy does not need to run feral to prove it is alive.

> Strength does not need to roar at every room. It knows what it can hold.

> Go quiet enough to hear yourself, then come back with the lantern.

> The wheel turns. Your job is to stop clinging to the spoke that is leaving.

> Justice says stop decorating the truth and sign your name under what is real.

> Hang still long enough for the truth to rearrange the room.

> Death is not doom. It is the door closing because the room is done.

> Temperance says do not rush the alchemy. Some things curdle when forced.

> The Devil does not own you; it studies where you hand over the keys.

## UI Copy Principles

**Labels and eyebrows** (all-caps, Bebas Neue, 10–11px, wide letter-spacing):
- Short noun phrases only: `MOON CURRENT` · `ELEMENTAL WEATHER` · `SACRED CHAOS` · `STRANGE RECORD`
- No verbs. No articles. No punctuation.
- Should feel like the handwritten label on a jar in a herbalist's cabinet.

**Button text:**
- Action verbs, no decoration: `Draw Card` · `Save Entry` · `Reroll` · `Enter the Grimoire`
- Not: `✨ Draw Your Mystical Card ✨`

**Placeholder text (textarea):**
- First-person fragments or questions: `What moved through you today...` · `Note the image that stayed.`
- Not: `Enter your journal entry here.`

**Empty state copy:**
- Honest, brief: `Nothing recorded yet.` · `The archive is empty.`
- Not: `Your magical journey begins when you write your first entry!`

## Relationship to the Aesthetic

The parchment, the botanical borders, the subtle rotations, the aged gradients — these create **the container.** The voice fills it. They should feel like the same hand.

The design says: *old, hand-made, held.*
The voice says: *true, unsparing, witnessed.*

Together they say: *this is a real practice, held by someone who takes it seriously.*

Neither performs. Both simply are.
